About Me

Hello! My name is Irtaza, and I am a 16-year-old high school student from Islamabad, Pakistan. I am passionate about everything tech and love trying out new technologies. When I am not coding or tinkering with electronics, I enjoy editing videos, and playing table tennis. I also love nature and Pokémon. I'm always happy to talk about computers and tech. Welcome to my portfolio!
